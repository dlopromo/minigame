# 冚棉胎 Rules

冚棉胎 is a shared-deck reaction game.

- Active player flips one card to the central pile.
- If the last two cards share the same rank, the pile becomes slappable.
- Any seated non-AI player may slap while `snapOpen` is true.
- Correct slap gains the pile size as score and clears the pile.
- Wrong slap loses 1 score.
- Highest score wins after the deck and pile are exhausted.
