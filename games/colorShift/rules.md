# 轉色牌 Rules

轉色牌 is an UNO-like original card game.

- Players receive 7 cards.
- Top discard and active color decide legal plays.
- Number cards match by color or value.
- Action cards match by color or action type.
- Wild cards can always be played and choose a new active color.
- First player to empty their hand wins.

Actions:

- `skip`: next player is skipped.
- `reverse`: turn direction reverses; in 2-player games it acts like skip.
- `draw2`: next player draws 2 and is skipped.
- `wild`: choose a color.
- `wild4`: next player draws 4 and is skipped.
