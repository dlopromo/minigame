# 鬥地主 Edge Cases

- Rocket beats all hands.
- Bomb beats non-bomb, non-rocket hands and can beat lower bombs.
- Four-with-two-singles and four-with-two-pairs are distinct shapes.
- Straights and chains cannot include `2` or jokers.
- Farmers should avoid covering teammates unless strategically useful.
- Four-player 鬥地主 is out of scope.
