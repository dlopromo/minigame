# 鋤大DEE Scoring

Base stake is 1.

Loser card multiplier:

- remaining fewer than 8: cards x 1
- remaining 8 or more: cards x 2
- remaining 10 or more: cards x 3
- remaining 13: cards x 4

Top-card / 頂大 violations are recorded and included in result calculations where
implemented. AI should defend with the strongest legal response when the next
player has one card remaining.
