# Blackjack Rules

21點 is a multi-player table where each player individually plays against the dealer.

## Card Values

- A is 11 or 1.
- J/Q/K are 10.
- Number cards use their rank.

## Player Actions

- Hit: draw one card.
- Stand: stop drawing.
- Bust: hand value exceeds 21 and the turn immediately advances.

## Dealer

After all players have stood, busted, or started with Blackjack:

- Dealer reveals hidden card.
- Dealer draws until hand value is at least 17.
- Dealer can bust.

## Results

- Blackjack beats non-Blackjack dealer hand.
- Player bust loses.
- Dealer bust means all non-bust players win.
- Equal value is push.
